Privacy

Rogue 2048 is a small game made by one person. This page says plainly what it stores and what it sends. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage. It does not use cookies.

  • A random player ID, created the first time you play. It is not your name and is not tied to an account.
  • Your display name, if you set one.
  • Your best score and best runs, a short history of your runs, your sound setting, and which hints and tutorial you have seen.
  • Daily runs waiting to be sent when you are back online, and a note of the Daily result you already sent.
  • The challenge links you have made or played, and your results on them.
  • A small error log kept on your device for debugging. It is never sent anywhere.

Clearing your browser data deletes all of this, and you will get a new player ID next time.

What is sent to the server

Only when you play the Daily, set your name, create or open a challenge, or look at a leaderboard, the game talks to its server. Depending on what you do, this includes: your player ID, your display name, the moves of the run (so the server can replay it and check that it is real), and the challenge code.

The server keeps: Daily runs with their moves and final board, your display name next to your player ID, and the challenges you create. Opening or accepting a challenge sends only the challenge code and which of the two it was.

Leaderboards publicly show a display name, points, moves and the highest tile. A display name is either generated for you or one you choose (3 to 16 letters, numbers, spaces, dashes or underscores, checked against a filter). Please do not use your real name or anything personal.

Anonymous totals

The site counts how often a few things happen each day: the page opened, a run started, a run ended, the share button tapped, a challenge opened, a challenge accepted. These are plain daily totals. They carry no ID and nothing links them to you.

What the game does not do

No cookies. No ads. No third-party analytics or tracking scripts. No selling of your data, and no sharing of it with anyone except the hosting providers named below. The font is served from this site, not from a font service.

Hosting and abuse protection

The site is hosted on Vercel and the game data is stored with Supabase in Mumbai. These providers keep their own server logs, which can include IP addresses and browser details. The game does not read those logs.

To slow down cheating and spam, the server also keeps short-lived counters tied to the IP address a request came from. For one of these limits the counter also includes your player ID. They are never shown publicly. They are removed automatically once they are older than two days; the cleanup runs as the game is used, so a record can sometimes stay a little longer.

Your choices

You can clear your browser data at any time. To ask for your leaderboard entries or display name to be removed, email the address below with your display name and roughly when you played. There are no accounts, so entries can only be found by display name and date.
